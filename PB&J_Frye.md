# My PB and J Robot

## Assumptions
- An unobstructed counter exists with the following items laid out:
 - Jar of PB
 - Jar of Jelly
 - Two slices of bread on a plate side by side and placed flat
 - Clean Butter knife set beside the plate on the counter
 - Napkin/Paper Towel
 - Robot is charged and ready


### Numbered Steps
 1. Remove the lids off the Peanut Butter and Jelly
 2. Pick up the butter knife
 3. Scoop the knife into the PB
 4. Spread the PB onto the leftmost piece of bread
 5. Loop - Gently spread the PB until the leftmost bread slice's upward facing surface is totally covered.
 6. Pick up the clean napkins and scoop the excess peanut butter from the sides of the leftmost bread slice.
 7. Scoop the knife into the Jelly
 8. Spread the jelly onto the rightmost piece of bread
 9. Loop - Gently spread the Jelly until the leftmmost bread slice's upward facing surface is totally covered.
 10. Pick up the napkin and scoop the excess jelly from both the rightmost bread slice and the butter knife
 11. Place the butter knife down
 12. Replace the lids on both the PB and Jelly jars
 13. Place bread together with PB and Jelly sides facing each other
 14. Throw away used napkins
 15. Place the PB and Jelly back in their original locations
